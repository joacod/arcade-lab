# Rekindled creative prompts

## Shared character specification

Production transparent-background 2D game sprite sheets for a premium modern dark-fantasy action game. Newly illustrated hand-painted/pre-rendered characters with dimensional materials, sculpted anatomy, warm rim lighting and cool fill, and expressive readable silhouettes. No pixel art, vector icons or enlarged retro sprites. Consistent elevated three-quarter orthographic camera looking down about 30 degrees. All cels face screen right and slightly toward the viewer. Full connected body drawn independently in each cel, no floor or cast shadow, no scenery, labels or grid lines.

1536 × 1024, four evenly spaced columns by two rows, 384 × 512 source cells. Feet share a cell-relative ground line around 88% down, bodies centered horizontally, complete silhouettes and equipment inside their cells with padding. Keep costume, proportions, camera, lighting and rendered size consistent across all eight cels. Broad color masses and restrained detail should remain readable around 65 pixels tall.

## Keeper sheet

A modern evolution of the Ember Ruins blue dungeon knight: heroic silver-blue armor, closed steel helmet with a narrow turquoise visor, swept-back crimson plume, burnished brass fittings, teal quilted tabard, dark leather boots, crimson half-cape, compact silver sword and small warm belt lantern. Compact heroic proportions with head about one quarter height, connected substantial armor and pale steel focal silhouette, no horns. Top row: four subtle breathing/settling idle cels. Bottom row: four distinct sequential running strides, right-foot contact, passing, left-foot contact and opposite passing, with alternating arms and cape response.

## Goblin and charger sheet

Two related enemies based on Ember Ruins' green goblins. Top row: four running cels of a hunched olive-green scavenger with expressive face, pointed ears, sharp ivory teeth, amber eyes, ragged desaturated teal hood and tunic, leather straps, wiry substantial legs and jagged iron cleaver. Bottom row: four running cels of a lean armored charger with the same goblin lineage, larger hunched shoulders, red-brown leather armor, a red hood with two long swept-back scarlet crest spikes and hooked forearm blades. Distinguish the charger by silhouette and costume. Use contact, passing, opposite contact and opposite passing strides with stable ground anchors. No skull masks, knight or demon.

## Elite sheet

A modern evolution of Ember Ruins' large red demon: hulking crimson warlord with huge black curved horns, carved basalt shoulder armor, bronze clasps, powerful connected musculature, fanged maw with molten orange core, ember fissures at the chest, massive clawed hands, thick digitigrade legs, heavy hooves and ragged burgundy loincloth. No held weapon. Broad imposing triangular silhouette, memorable face and hands, restrained molten detail. Top row: four breathing/menacing idle cels. Bottom row: four distinct heavy advancing walk strides, alternating foot contacts and passing poses. Keep horns, claws and feet inside every cell.

Production 2D game ground plate, 1536 × 1024. Perfect overhead orthographic view of fully walkable medieval ruin ground, edge to edge. Weathered blue-gray limestone flagstones of varied rectangular shapes, moss between joints, a little earth and fallen gold leaves near corners, and a subtle worn central circular inlay. Painterly mineral texture and soft bevels with quiet contrast, desaturated teal slate and warm stone undertones. Broad cool moonlight from upper right and faint warm amber spill near left and right edges. Leave value separation for small actors and red warnings.
