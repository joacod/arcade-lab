# Ember Ruins artwork

**16x16 DungeonTileset II v1.7** by **0x72 / Robert**, from the [official asset page](https://0x72.itch.io/dungeontileset-ii), under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

`dungeon.png` is the 512 × 512 atlas, renamed from `0x72_DungeonTilesetII_v1.7.png`. `tile-list.txt` and `upstream-readme.txt` preserve its frame metadata and setup instructions.

The art definition in `src/games/last-light-clearing/themes.ts` maps knight_f, goblin and big_demon to four-frame idle/run clips, mirrored horizontally for facing. Flashes and defeat rings are procedural effects. Ground uses floor_1 through floor_4; masonry, columns, crates and the red wall-fountain animation decorate the perimeter outside the traversable arena.
